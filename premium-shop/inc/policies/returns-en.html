1. Returns without giving a reason ({days} days)

You can send an item back without giving a reason within {days} days of receiving it. This right of return is a voluntary commitment of {company}: Swiss law provides no right of withdrawal for online purchases.

  • The item must be new, unused, complete (accessories, manuals, cables) and in its original packaging.
  • Games, software and shrink-wrapped accessories must be returned unopened.
  • Excluded: gift cards and digital codes once delivered or activated, and headsets or earphones whose sealed packaging has been opened (hygiene).
  • If you ordered the wrong model by mistake, this is also a return without a reason.

2. Faulty item

  • Fault found on arrival or within {days} days: tell us within {days} days of receipt. We send you a new item or refund you in full, shipping costs included.
  • Fault appearing later: all our products have a 2-year warranty from the delivery date. Contact us as soon as you notice the fault: we have the item repaired or exchanged; if that is not possible, we refund you.
  • For consoles and graphics cards, a short video or photo of the problem speeds things up.

3. Wrong item, missing item or order mistake

  • You received a different item from the one you ordered, an item is missing or the quantity is wrong: tell us within 7 days of receipt.
  • We send you the correct item within 2 business days, without waiting for the wrong item to come back, or refund you in full if you prefer.
  • Parcel damaged in transit: tell us within 48 hours with photos of the parcel and the item. If the damage is visible on delivery, you can also refuse the parcel.

4. How to return an item

  1. Write to us at {email}{phone_text} with your order number, the item and the reason (with photos for a fault or damage).
  2. We reply within 1 business day with a prepaid return label and instructions.
  3. Pack the item carefully and drop the parcel at the post office within 7 days of receiving the label.

Please do not send items back without contacting us first: without an order number we cannot match them.

5. Refunds: when do you get your money?

  • Item check: within 3 business days of it arriving at our premises. We confirm the refund by e-mail.
  • Refund issued: at the latest 5 business days after we receive the returned item (never later than 14 days).
  • Money back on your account, depending on the payment method:
    • TWINT, PayPal, PostFinance: 1 to 3 business days;
    • credit or debit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay: 3 to 10 business days depending on your bank;
    • bank transfer: 1 to 3 business days (we ask for your IBAN).
  • Amount refunded: the price of the item. The original shipping costs are refunded too for a faulty or wrong item, a mistake on our side, or when you return the whole order.
  • Refunds always go to the payment method used for the order. You can also choose an exchange or a voucher.
